Modern Student Welfare Platform — UniSphere
UniSphere is a centralized digital platform designed to streamline and enhance student life and academic operations in educational institutions. It offers a comprehensive suite of tools and services that support students, faculty, and administration in one unified system.

 Core Features:
  Timetable & Exam Scheduling:
  Manage and access class timetables and examination schedules efficiently.

Attendance Tracking:
  Real-time student attendance system for streamlined academic monitoring.

Performance Analytics:
  Visualize and analyze student academic data for better decision-making.

Smart Additions:
  AI-Powered Smart Assistant:
An intelligent chatbot to answer student queries related to academics, campus life, and more.

Hostel Leave Management System (Improved):
  Digital process for students to apply and track hostel leave requests with admin approval.

Art & Creativity Showcase:
  A dedicated gallery for students to exhibit their artwork, photography, poetry, and creative projects.

Student Marketplace:
  Platform for students to buy, sell, or exchange second-hand books, notes, and essentials.

Campus Navigation Map:
  Interactive map to help newcomers and visitors explore the campus with ease.
